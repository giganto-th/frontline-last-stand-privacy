Privacy Policy: Frontline: Last Stand

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Frontline: Last Stand ("the game") is made by Giganto.

Information we collect

The game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not connect to the internet while you play.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps the following only on your device, in storage that other apps cannot read:

  • your settings (controls, sound, language, graphics quality);
  • your high score and best wave;
  • a play-test log (waves reached, accuracy, frame rate) used to improve the game.

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to copy the play-test log yourself (Stats → Copy Playtest Log) and send it to someone. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: kimookpong@gmail.com
